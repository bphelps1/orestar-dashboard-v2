## Acquisition notes

- All 414 non-placeholder portraits in the 451-record Capitol Club snapshot were collected. The remaining 37 directory records publish generic avatars; some received verified official-site replacements.
- Eliza Walton appears twice in the directory. Contact 875 retains its directory portrait; contact 876 received the official Oregon Environmental Council portrait after its directory email domain matched that organization. The duplicate records were not merged.
- Some official sources retain older images. Roger Beyer’s source filename includes 2009; Jeff Stone’s official association article is from 2020 and its image URL includes 2017. Those are source dates, not verified dates when the photos were taken.
- The supplied Google spreadsheet disables copying and downloading, so it was not used as an image extraction source.

- Collection resumed with five seconds between image requests, checkpointing each successful file. The earlier rate limit was respected; no proxies or authentication bypasses were used.
- Capitol Club source: <https://oregoncapitolclub.org/user/>. Each catalog entry includes its exact source image and profile URL.
- Two oversized camera JPEGs were recovered using JPEG decoder downsampling before pixel loading, preserving EXIF orientation and the decoded-pixel limit.
- Future directory refreshes retain the reviewed official-site portraits and stop on HTTP 429. The refresh workflow is manual and opens an asset PR.
- Tests cover ID matching, source/path validation, missing images, official portrait preservation, large JPEG orientation, and Excel image embedding after save/reopen.
